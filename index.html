Which calendar files can I import?

Imports single-day and overnight events, local recurring rules, and exceptions. Foreign time zones are converted for single events. Foreign-zone series, yearly rules, RDATE, and multi-day spans are reported without importing. Alarms and attendees are not imported. Export uses local wall-clock times. JSON remains the complete backup format. iCalendar format.

Examples and supported phrases

“Dentist tomorrow at 2pm”, “Standup every Monday and Wednesday at 9am after 10 times”, “Review last Friday of the month at 3pm until 2027-12-31”, “Holiday 2026-12-25 all day”. Add #Category at the end and notes: followed by details. Dates default to today, times to 9–10am; always review before saving.
